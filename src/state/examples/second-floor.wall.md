# Buckley-Watanabe residence · second floor

418 Cedar St, Leavenworth. Second-floor walls of the main house, grids C to F, from the second-floor plan on A101 (wall lines read from the drawing), window and door schedules A007, levels A200 and the roof framing plan S2.3 (Harezlak Engineering, Rev. 1). Nothing over the garage is included: the unit above it is framed separately.

The house is 34'-8 3/4" north to south and 24'-0 5/8" east to west as built, outside of framing (south wall to the far side of the fire wall on C, west wall to east wall), against 34'-6 1/16" and 24'-0" read from the drawings. The walls are placed from the southwest corner as drawn, so the extra length is at the north end and the extra width on the east side, and openings and interior walls keep their drawn positions measured from the south and west.

The walls are 8'-1 1/4" from the second floor (10'-1 7/8") to the top of the second-floor wall (18'-3 1/8"), A200. Roof trusses at 24" O.C. span east to west and bear on the east and west walls (S2.3); the interior walls are non-structural, tied to the trusses per 4/S4.3. Exterior headers are 4x8 per S2.3 plan note 4, except the two 4x12s called out on the east wall. Window heads are measured off the elevations: the 54" units top out 82 7/8" above the floor, so with the 3/8" allowance the rough openings head out at 6'-11 1/4".

Exterior walls are seen from inside. Interior walls are seen from the south or west: east-west walls run west to east, north-south walls run north to south. Walls are listed in the order they are stood: the east and west walls run through the corners and go up first, then the north and south walls between them, then the interior walls. Top and bottom plates come in 16' lengths, so no piece is longer than that. Plan positions are measured east and north from the outside corner of the framing at the southwest (grids 1 and F), to each wall's left end on the face it is seen from.

The west wall along the stair is the same two-story balloon wall left out of the first floor, so it is left out here too, with the stair window 12 in it and the southwest bedroom's north wall where it meets it. Closet fronts with no wall over them (closet H by the hall and closet K by the southwest bedroom) just get a header between the walls either side. Verify in the field.
